Loft Conversions in Surrey

Surrey homeowners are making a straightforward calculation: the cost of moving to a larger property — stamp duty, agent fees, legal costs, disruption — often exceeds the cost of a well-executed loft conversion. With property values across the county ranging from £600k in Woking to well over £1.5M in Cobham and Oxshott, the financial case for converting rather than moving is compelling.

The demand for additional space has also shifted in character. Since hybrid working became the norm, the home office has become one of the most requested loft uses across Surrey. Families in Twickenham and Surbiton are adding nurseries and children's rooms. Couples in Weybridge and Esher are creating master suites and dressing rooms. The brief varies — but the underlying need is the same: more considered, better-designed space.

Surrey's housing stock is well suited to loft conversion. Victorian and Edwardian terraces dominate in Kingston, Surbiton, and the inner Surrey towns. Larger Edwardian and inter-war semis and detached properties are common across Weybridge, Esher, and Cobham. Most properties have traditional cut timber roofs, good ridge heights, and manageable rear elevations — the foundations of a straightforward conversion.

Local Planning Notes

Planning Permission for Loft Conversions in Surrey

The majority of Surrey loft conversions proceed under permitted development rights — no planning application required. Most rear dormers on houses that have not exhausted their permitted development allowance qualify automatically, provided they meet standard size and material conditions.

That said, Surrey covers multiple local planning authorities — each with its own policies, conservation areas, and sensitivities. Knowing which authority covers your property, and what local restrictions apply, is an important first step.

Surrey Planning Authorities We Work With

Elmbridge Borough Council

Weybridge · Esher · Cobham · Walton on Thames · Thames Ditton · Molesey

Generally permissive outside Green Belt. Several riverside conservation areas where full planning consent is required.

Kingston upon Thames

Kingston · Surbiton · New Malden

London Borough context. Different permitted development rules can apply in some wards and conservation areas.

Woking Borough Council

Woking · Chobham · Pyrford

Mix of urban and Green Belt locations. Most properties are suitable under standard permitted development conditions.

Runnymede Borough Council

Weybridge (parts) · Chertsey · Addlestone

Green Belt boundaries may influence what can be built externally. Loft works within roof volume are usually straightforward.

Surrey Heath Borough Council

Camberley · Lightwater · Chobham · Ascot (parts)

Suburban and semi-rural mix. Standard roof extensions are commonly acceptable where Article 4 restrictions do not apply.

Windsor & Maidenhead

Ascot · Sunningdale · Sunninghill

Character and heritage constraints can be stronger in selected zones, but compliant rear dormers remain feasible in many streets.

Permitted Development: When No Application Is Needed

  • ✓Rear dormers within volume limits (40m3 terraced, 50m3 detached)
  • ✓Materials similar to existing property
  • ✓Does not exceed original ridge height
  • ✓Property not in a conservation area or listed
  • ✓Article 4 direction not in force for your property

Planning Permission Required When

  • ✗Property is within a designated conservation area
  • ✗Property is a listed building
  • ✗Front or side dormers proposed
  • ✗Dormer exceeds permitted development volume limits
  • ✗Green Belt designation applies (rare but worth confirming)
