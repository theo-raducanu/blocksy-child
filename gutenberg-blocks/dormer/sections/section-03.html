Dormer Loft Conversions

What Is a Dormer Loft Conversion?

A dormer loft conversion adds a vertical extension — a dormer roof extension — to your existing roof slope, creating full-height walls, a proper floor, and significantly more natural light. Unlike a Velux conversion, a dormer projects outward from the roofline, giving you a genuinely usable room.

This type of loft conversion with dormer windows is London's most popular choice, particularly on Victorian and Edwardian terraces across Zones 2–5. A rear dormer typically qualifies for permitted development — no planning permission required.

Is a Dormer Right for Your Property?

  • ✓At least 2.2m headroom at the ridge (highest point of roof)
  • ✓Victorian or Edwardian terrace, semi-detached, or detached house
  • ✓Traditional timber-framed roof structure
  • ✓Rear elevation — not in a conservation area (or willing to apply)
